Privacy policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Short version: Digital Dash works without an account and then keeps everything on your device. If you sign in, your dashboard is saved to this site's database so it syncs between your devices, and only you can read it. Nothing is sold or used for ads.

Without an account

Tasks, events, timers, balances, settings and photos you add are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent anywhere.

With an account

  • Sign-in. You can sign in with an email link or with Google. With Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to identify your account.
  • Your dashboard. Your dashboard data (tasks, events, courses, balances, goals, settings and similar) is stored in a database hosted by Supabase in Canada. Database rules make each account's data readable only by that account.
  • Photos. Background and widget images are stored in a private storage area that only your account can access.
  • Reminders. If you turn on reminders, your device's push address and upcoming reminder times and titles are stored so notifications can be sent. They are delivered through your browser's push service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla).
  • AI helper. Only when you use it, the titles, urgency and due dates of the tasks involved, and for week planning the titles of your next 7 days of events, are sent to Anthropic's AI to make a plan. We count requests per account to enforce a daily limit.
  • Study rooms. When you join a room, people with the same room code see your display name, whether you are focusing and, if you choose, your top task. Room activity is not stored.

Other services

  • Spotify. If you connect Spotify, the sign-in tokens stay on your device and are used only to show what is playing.
  • Fonts. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Your choices

  • Download a copy of your data at any time from the account menu.
  • Delete your account from the account menu. This removes your saved dashboard, photos, reminders and devices from the database.
  • Signing out can also clear the data on that device.
