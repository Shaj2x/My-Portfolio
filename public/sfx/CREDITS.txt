Sound effects in this folder

Built by scripts/sounds/build.py, which lists every sound, its source and its loudness target.

- earshot by Leon Lin (https://github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot), MIT licence. Sounds made in code from
  its bank with `earshot sfx render`: the room's switches, speaker and blind, the keyboard's
  keys, and Ma's Garden's marimba, glockenspiel, whoosh, thump and shimmer.
- uisfx by Romain Simon (https://uisfx.com), audio released under CC0 1.0, as vendored in
  Clipwing's real-sfx skill (https://github.com/Clipwing/skills). Interface cues (send, receive,
  complete, achievement, wake), the plushie's squeak and Ma's Garden's dreamy cues.

The perfume spray is designed in build.py: earshot's micro click for the pump, then a steady
bright hiss of mist made from filtered noise.

All sounds were trimmed, loudness-matched by group and encoded to MP3 for the web.
